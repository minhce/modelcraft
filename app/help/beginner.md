New to 3D? The **Beginner** menu style puts a few big buttons on the first tab, so you can build things from shapes and holes before you learn to draw faces and push them up. Choose it in {{ui:Settings}} ({{key:Mod+,}}) › {{ui:ModelCraft}} › {{ui:Menus, keys and mouse}} › {{ui:Beginner}}. Every other style stays as it was; switch back at any time.

## Build with shapes

1. Click a shape on the {{ui:Make}} tab: {{ui:Box}}, {{ui:Cylinder}}, {{ui:Sphere}}, {{ui:Cone}} or {{ui:Roof}}. Each is 20 mm across.
2. Click where it goes: on the ground, or on a face of another shape (it stands on that face). {{key:Esc}} stops.
3. Click a shape to select it, then {{ui:Move}}, {{ui:Rotate}} or {{ui:Scale}} it. Type a size while you move or scale to be exact.

## Make holes

A hole is a shape that cuts into the shapes it is grouped with.

- Drop a {{ui:Box hole}} or a {{ui:Round hole}} (they are grey), or select any shape and click {{ui:Make it a hole}}. {{ui:Make it solid}} turns a hole back into a shape.
- Place the hole where it should cut: through a shape, or partly into it.
- Select the shapes and the holes ({{key:Shift}}+click adds one), then click {{ui:Group}}. The holes are cut out and the result is one part. {{key:Mod+Z}} takes it apart again.

With no hole in the selection, {{ui:Group}} makes an ordinary group.

## Line things up

Select two or more shapes, then use the align buttons: their arrows list {{ui:Align left}} / {{ui:Align centers (red)}} / {{ui:Align right}}, the same along green (front, middle, back) and blue (bottoms, middles, tops). They line up with the outside of the whole selection. {{ui:Drop to the ground}} sets each selected shape back on the ground.

## Check it for printing

{{ui:Check for 3D printing…}} on the {{ui:Make}} tab checks the model and downloads it for your printer. See [Get a model ready for 3D printing](topic:print-3d) and [Supports, wall thickness, hollowing and repair](topic:print-tools).

## The other tabs

{{ui:View}} has the camera: orbit, pan, zoom, zoom to everything, and the standard views. {{ui:More tools}} has every other ModelCraft tool, small. With this style the **right** mouse button orbits and the **middle** button pans; the wheel zooms.
